The first three WeChat comments are estimated to be from November 23, 2026 (Beijing time). The exact posting times are unknown.

  1. Choosing models and tools by task

    One commenter described using different models and tools for coordination, front-end work, back-end work, computer use and batch processing. Their takeaway was to choose a model and agent environment that fit each task.

  2. Trying AI suggestions on a short essay

    Another commenter initially asked AI to adjust the format of a short personal essay. They also tried its writing suggestions and chose to keep one sentence.

  3. Coordinating multiple agents in Antigravity Swarm

    A commenter described using Neon for orchestration, Hydrogen for execution, and Carbon or Opus for review and architecture. They found the dashboard useful for coordinating sub-agents across several projects and Git worktrees.

    They preferred this workflow to their experience with multiple Codex sessions. This comparison reflects one person's experience. They also planned to try T3 Code and Orca.

  4. Batching video and 3D generation from Antigravity

    A WeChat commenter suggested having Antigravity drive the Google Flow interface to generate videos in batches and the Tripo3D interface to generate 3D models in batches. The comment date is unknown. The combined workflow has not been tested.
